YouTube Music keyboard shortcuts on Mac: the complete list

Updated 8 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: in YouTube Music on a Mac, press Space to play or pause, J for the next song, K for the previous one, L / H to jump 10 seconds, = / - for volume and / to search. Press ? to show the full list. These work while the YouTube Music window is in front; the media keys work from any app.

Shortcuts that work from any app

The single-letter shortcuts above only work while YouTube Music is the front window. To control it while you're in another app, use your keyboard's media keys ⏮ ▶︎❚❚ ⏭, AirPods or Control Center. If the play key opens Apple Music instead, read how to fix the media keys.

FAQ

What is the shortcut for next song in YouTube Music on Mac?

Press J (or Shift + N) for the next song and K (or Shift + P) for the previous one while YouTube Music is in front. From other apps, use the media keys or Control Center.

How do I see all YouTube Music shortcuts?

Press ? (Shift + /) on music.youtube.com or in an app like Tubemac. YouTube Music shows its full shortcut overview.

Why don't the YouTube Music shortcuts work?

The letter shortcuts only work while YouTube Music is the active window and no text field (like the search bar) is selected. Click an empty area of the page and try again.
