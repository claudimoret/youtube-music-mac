Can't sign in to YouTube Music in a Mac app? Here's the fix

Updated 8 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: macOS only offers passkeys and saved passwords to Safari and to apps that Google has officially linked to its sign-in. In other apps – including Tubemac and most YouTube Music apps – choose Try another way → Enter your password on the Google sign-in page. You only need to do this once; the app keeps you signed in.

Sign in step by step

  1. In the app, click Sign in (top right) and enter your Google email address.
  2. If Google asks for your passkey, choose Try another way.
  3. Choose Enter your password. Need your password? Open the Passwords app on your Mac, search for “google” and copy it.
  4. Confirm the sign-in on your phone if Google asks for 2-Step Verification.
  5. Done – YouTube Music opens with your library and Premium, and stays signed in next time.

Why passkeys don't work here

A passkey is tied to a website, like google.com. macOS only hands it to apps it can trust for that website: Safari, other browsers, and apps that Google has officially approved. A YouTube Music app made by someone else can't get that approval, so the passkey request fails or shows an error. That's a security feature of macOS, not a bug in the app.

The same applies to automatic password suggestions: they only appear in Safari and approved apps.

Still not working?

  • “This browser or app may not be secure”: Google blocks sign-in from some embedded browsers. Tubemac identifies itself like Safari, so this normally doesn't appear. If it does, try again later or use the Safari web app (File → Add to Dock), where passkeys work.
  • Signed out after a while? Google occasionally asks you to sign in again for security. Just repeat the steps above.
  • Wrong account? Click your profile picture in YouTube Music → Switch account.

FAQ

Why can't I use my passkey to sign in to YouTube Music in a Mac app?

macOS only gives passkeys for google.com to Safari, browsers and apps Google has approved. In other apps choose Try another way → Enter your password on the Google sign-in page.

Do I have to sign in every time?

No. Apps like Tubemac keep you signed in after the first sign-in, just like a browser does.

Where do I find my Google password on a Mac?

Open the Passwords app (or System Settings → Passwords on older macOS), search for “google” and copy the password from there.
