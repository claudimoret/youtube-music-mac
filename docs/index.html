Up and running in two minutes

Tubemac isn't sold through the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. That's normal for free apps from the web.

Download

Download Tubemac, double-click the file to unzip it and drag Tubemac into your Applications folder.

Allow it once

Open Tubemac. If macOS says it can't be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Sign in & listen

Click Sign in and log in with your Google account. Tubemac remembers you – next time it opens straight to your music.

Questions & answers

Is there a YouTube Music app for Mac?

Google doesn't make one – on macOS, YouTube Music officially only runs in the browser. Tubemac fills that gap: it puts the official YouTube Music player into a real Mac window with media keys, Control Center controls and background playback. It's free, 1 MB, and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 or later.

What's the best YouTube Music app for Mac?

If you want something that feels native and stays out of your way, Tubemac is the lightest option at 1 MB. YTMDesktop and Pear Desktop run on Windows, Mac and Linux and add extras like Discord status or plugins, but each is about 118 MB because they bring their own Chrome browser. Kaset rebuilds the interface in Apple's style but needs macOS 15.4 and Premium for full playback. See the full comparison.

Is Tubemac free? Are there ads?

Tubemac is completely free and open source. It adds no ads and no tracking. With a free YouTube Music account you'll see YouTube's usual ads, just like in the browser; with Premium you won't.

Do I need YouTube Music Premium?

No. Tubemac works with free and Premium accounts – it shows the same YouTube Music you know from music.youtube.com.

Why does macOS say it can't open Tubemac?

Apple asks for a paid developer membership to “notarize” apps. Tubemac is a free hobby project, so macOS asks you to confirm it once: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. After that it opens like any other app.

Why can't I sign in with my passkey?

macOS only offers passkeys and saved passwords to Safari and apps that Google has officially approved. On the Google sign-in page choose Try another way → Enter your password. You only need to do this once.

Is it safe?

Tubemac only talks to YouTube Music and Google's sign-in – nothing else. It has no analytics and no servers of its own, and the complete source code is public on GitHub.
