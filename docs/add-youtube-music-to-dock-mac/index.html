How to add YouTube Music to the Dock on your Mac

Updated 8 November 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: on macOS 14 Sonoma or later, open music.youtube.com in Safari and choose File → Add to Dock. In Chrome, use the install option in the address bar. For an icon that opens a real Mac app – with media keys, Control Center and background playback – install Tubemac and keep it in the Dock.

Option 1: Safari “Add to Dock” (built in)

  1. Open music.youtube.com in Safari and sign in.
  2. In the menu bar choose File → Add to Dock.
  3. Keep the name “YouTube Music” or change it, then click Add.
  4. Click the new icon in the Dock – YouTube Music opens in its own window without Safari's toolbar.

Good: nothing to download, passkeys and saved passwords work at sign-in. Less good: it's still the website in a window – closing it stops the music, and there's no Mac-specific polish.

Option 2: Install it from Chrome

  1. Open music.youtube.com in Chrome.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the ⋮ menu and choose the install option.
  3. While the app is open, right-click its Dock icon and choose Options → Keep in Dock.

Good: quick if you already use Chrome. Less good: needs Chrome installed, follows Chrome's look rather than macOS'.

Option 3: A real Mac app – Tubemac

  1. Download Tubemac (1 MB), unzip it and drag it into Applications.
  2. Open it once and confirm in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (why?).
  3. While it's running, right-click the icon in the Dock and choose Options → Keep in Dock.

Good: designed to feel like a Mac app – artwork runs edge to edge behind the window buttons, media keys and Control Center just work, and closing the window doesn't stop the music. Less good: passkeys don't work at sign-in; use your password once.

FAQ

How do I add YouTube Music to the Dock on a Mac?

In Safari on macOS 14 or later, open music.youtube.com and choose File → Add to Dock. Alternatively install it from Chrome, or download the free Tubemac app and keep it in the Dock.

Why don't I see “Add to Dock” in Safari?

Add to Dock needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. On older macOS versions, use the Chrome install option instead.

How do I remove the YouTube Music icon from the Dock?

Drag the icon out of the Dock until “Remove” appears, or right-click it and turn off Options → Keep in Dock. This only removes the shortcut, not the app.
