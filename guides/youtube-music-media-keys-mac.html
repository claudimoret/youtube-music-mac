Start a song in YouTube Music once – from then on the media keys, your AirPods and Control Center control it. A dedicated app like Tubemac makes this the most reliable, because YouTube Music is never just one of many browser tabs.

Why the play button opens Apple Music

macOS keeps track of one “Now Playing” app. The ▶︎❚❚ key, the skip keys, AirPods taps and the Control Center widget all go to that app. When nothing has played since you started your Mac, there is no Now Playing app – so macOS falls back to Apple's Music app and launches it.

That's why the problem usually shows up right after a restart, or after you closed the tab or app that was playing.

How to make the media keys control YouTube Music

  1. Open YouTube Music – in a browser tab, a Safari web app or an app like Tubemac.
  2. Start any song by clicking it once. This makes YouTube Music the Mac's Now Playing app.
  3. Use ▶︎❚❚, ⏮ and ⏭ on your keyboard, AirPods or Touch Bar. They now control YouTube Music, even while you work in another app.
  4. If the keys jump to another app later, play something in YouTube Music again – the most recent player always wins.

Common problems and fixes

The keys control a video in another tab

Browsers hand the media keys to whichever tab played last. A paused YouTube video, a podcast or a video call in another tab can “steal” them. Close those tabs, or keep YouTube Music in its own app window so it isn't competing with other tabs.

Control Center shows ±10 or 15-second buttons instead of next/previous

YouTube Music tells the Mac that it supports seeking backwards and forwards. Depending on the browser, the Now Playing widget then shows skip-seconds buttons instead of track buttons. Tubemac removes those seek controls, so Control Center shows back, play/pause and next plus a slider to jump within the song – like Apple Music.

Two YouTube Music entries in Control Center

This happens when YouTube Music is open twice, for example in a tab and in a web app. Close one of them.

Keyboard shortcuts inside YouTube Music

When the YouTube Music window is in front you can also use its own shortcuts, for example Space for play/pause and J / K for next/previous. See the full list in YouTube Music keyboard shortcuts on Mac.

FAQ

Why does my Mac's play button open Apple Music instead of YouTube Music?

macOS sends the play key to the app that played audio most recently. If YouTube Music hasn't played anything since you started your Mac, macOS opens Apple Music. Start a song in YouTube Music once and the key will control it.

Do AirPods controls work with YouTube Music on Mac?

Yes. AirPods taps and squeezes use the same Now Playing system as the keyboard's media keys, so they control YouTube Music once it is the app that played most recently.

How do I get next and previous buttons for YouTube Music in Control Center?

Some setups show 10- or 15-second skip buttons because YouTube Music registers seek controls. Tubemac replaces them with back, play/pause and next plus a song slider.
