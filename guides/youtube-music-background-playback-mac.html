Short answer: YouTube Music keeps playing as long as its page stays open – in a background tab, a minimized window or another Space. It stops the moment you close the tab or window. To close the window and keep listening, use an app like Tubemac: closing its window only hides it, and clicking the Dock icon brings it back.

How to listen in the background with Tubemac

  1. Download Tubemac from the Tubemac website and move it to your Applications folder.
  2. Open Tubemac, sign in with your Google account and start a song or playlist.
  3. Close the window with ⌘W or the red button. The music keeps playing and the app stays in the Dock.
  4. Control playback with your keyboard's media keys, AirPods or Control Center.
  5. Click the Tubemac icon in the Dock to bring the window back, or press ⌘Q to quit and stop the music.

Tips for uninterrupted listening

  • Keep it out of your tab clutter. In a browser, it's easy to close the YouTube Music tab by accident together with others. A separate app avoids that.
  • Keep it in the Dock. Right-click the app's Dock icon → Options → Keep in Dock, so it's always one click away.
  • Mind sleep. When your Mac goes to sleep, playback stops – that's the same for every app.

FAQ

Why does YouTube Music stop when I close the window on my Mac?

In a browser or web app, closing the window closes the page that plays the music. Tubemac works differently: closing the window only hides it, so the music keeps playing until you quit the app.

Does YouTube Music play in the background without Premium on a Mac?

Yes. On a Mac, YouTube Music keeps playing when its window is in the background or minimized, with free and Premium accounts. Background play as a Premium feature only applies to the phone apps.

How do I bring the Tubemac window back?

Click the Tubemac icon in the Dock, or choose Window → Tubemac in the menu bar.
